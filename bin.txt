## Neural crest and its main derivatives

::: {.columns}
::: {.column}

::: {.info-box}
Neural crest specification begins at the **neural plate border**, while the neural plate is still open.
:::

::: {.card}
After fusion, neural crest cells occupy the dorsal neural tube. They later delaminate and migrate to distant regions.
:::

:::
::: {.column}

::: {.card}
Major derivatives include:

- sensory and autonomic ganglia
- Schwann cells
- melanocytes
- the adrenal medulla
- craniofacial components
:::
